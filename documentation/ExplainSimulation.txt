================================================================================
IN-DEPTH SCHEMATIC ANALYSIS: SALLEN-KEY AUDIO FILTER (NE5532)
================================================================================

1. COMPLETE CIRCUIT OVERVIEW
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Signal Path:
Audio_Input -> HP Filter -> HP Op-Amp Stage -> LP Filter -> LP Buffer Stage -> Audio_Output

- High-Pass (HP) Section: Located on the left, comprising capacitors C1, C2 (150 nF each),
  resistors R3, R4 (5.6 kΩ each), dual-gang potentiometer VR1A/VR1B (50 kΩ), gain-setting 
  resistors R5 (1 kΩ) and R6 (1.8 kΩ), and the first NE5532 op-amp stage.
- Low-Pass (LP) Section: Located on the right, comprising dual-gang potentiometer 
  VR1A/VR1B (10 kΩ), series resistors R3, R4 (1 kΩ each), R5 (220 Ω), capacitors 
  C1, C2, C3 (10 nF each), and the second NE5532 op-amp stage configured as a voltage follower.
- Op-Amp Stages:
  1. HP Stage: Non-inverting Sallen-Key active filter with a gain of Av = 1.556 V/V (+3.84 dB).
  2. LP Stage: Unity-gain (Av = 1 V/V, 0 dB) non-inverting Sallen-Key buffer.
- Signal Direction: Left-to-right: Audio_Input -> HP filter -> HP op-amp output (pin 7) -> 
  LP filter -> LP buffer op-amp output (pin 7) -> Audio_Output.
- Frequency Control: HP cutoff is controlled by the left 50 kΩ dual pot; LP cutoff is 
  controlled by the right 10 kΩ dual pot.
- Section Interaction: The HP stage ends with an op-amp output (pin 7), providing near-zero 
  output impedance. This completely isolates the HP section from the LP section, preventing loading.
- Functional Breakdown:
  * Filtering: C1, C2, R3, R4, VR1 (HP); VR1, R3, R4, R5, C1, C2, C3 (LP).
  * Buffering/Amplification: U1B (HP) and U1B (LP).
  * Frequency Adjustment: Dual 50 kΩ pot (HP); Dual 10 kΩ pot (LP).
  * Biasing/Gain: R5 (1 kΩ) and R6 (1.8 kΩ) in HP stage.
  * Active Feedback: HP pin 7 back to VR1A (HP); LP pin 7 back to C1/C2 junction (LP).


2. HIGH-PASS (HP) SECTION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Component Breakdown:
- C1 (150 nF): AC coupling capacitor. Blocks DC offsets and attenuates low frequencies. 
  Connected between Audio_Input and Node HP1. Increasing value lowers fc.
- C2 (150 nF): Second reactive element forming the 2nd pole (-12 dB/octave). Connected 
  between Node HP1 and Pin 5 (+ input). Increasing value lowers fc.
- R3 (5.6 kΩ): Sets minimum resistance for first RC branch. Connected between Node HP1 
  and VR1A terminal. Limits maximum cutoff frequency.
- R4 (5.6 kΩ): Sets minimum resistance for second RC branch. Connected between Pin 5 
  and VR1B terminal. Prevents non-inverting input from shorting directly.
- VR1A (50 kΩ Dual Pot, A): Variable resistor in rheostat mode. Connected between R3 
  and HP pin 7 feedback line. Adjusts cutoff frequency.
- VR1B (50 kΩ Dual Pot, B): Variable resistor in rheostat mode. Connected between R4 
  and R5/R6 divider node. Adjusts second pole frequency simultaneously.
- R5 (1 kΩ): Feedback resistor. Connected between pin 7 (output) and pin 6 (- input). 
  Sets op-amp closed-loop gain along with R6.
- R6 (1.8 kΩ): Ground gain resistor. Connected between pin 6 (- input) and ground. 
  Sets gain and Q factor.
- U1B / NE5532: Operational amplifier. Provides +3.84 dB gain, active Sallen-Key 
  bootstrapping, and low output impedance.

HP Signal Path Step-by-Step:
1. Audio_Input feeds full AC audio spectrum.
2. Signal passes through C1 to Node HP1; low frequencies encounter high capacitive reactance.
3. Active feedback from op-amp pin 7 injects energy via VR1A + R3 into Node HP1 (sharpens knee).
4. Signal passes through C2 to Node HP2 (Pin 5); further attenuated below cutoff.
5. Signal enters U1B non-inverting input (Pin 5).
6. Op-amp U1B amplifies by 1.556x (+3.84 dB) and outputs at Pin 7 to drive the LP section.


3. HP CUTOFF FREQUENCY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Transfer Function:
H(s) = (Av * s^2) / (s^2 + s * ((3 - Av) / (R * C)) + 1 / (R^2 * C^2))

Cutoff Formula:
fc = 1 / (2 * pi * R * C)

Where R = R3 + VR1A = R4 + VR1B, and C = C1 = C2 = 150 nF.

Mathematical Derivation / Values:
- C = 150 nF = 1.5e-7 F
- R_min = 5.6 kΩ = 5600 Ω
- R_max = 5.6 kΩ + 50 kΩ = 55.6 kΩ = 55,600 Ω

Calculations:
- Max Cutoff (VR1 = 0 Ω):
  fc_max = 1 / (2 * pi * 5600 * 1.5e-7) ≈ 189.4 Hz
- Min Cutoff (VR1 = 50 kΩ):
  fc_min = 1 / (2 * pi * 55600 * 1.5e-7) ≈ 19.1 Hz

Assumptions: Ideal op-amp, perfect dual-potentiometer resistance tracking, zero source impedance.


4. HP OP-AMP STAGE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Inputs: Pin 5 = Non-inverting (+), Pin 6 = Inverting (-).
- Feedback: Negative feedback through R5 (1 kΩ) to Pin 6; positive active feedback from 
  Pin 7 to VR1A.
- Configuration: Non-inverting amplifier with active Sallen-Key feedback.
- Gain Derivation:
  Av = 1 + (R5 / R6) = 1 + (1000 / 1800) = 1.5556 V/V (+3.84 dB)
- Quality Factor (Q):
  Q = 1 / (3 - Av) = 1 / (3 - 1.5556) = 1 / 1.4444 ≈ 0.692 ≈ 0.707 (Butterworth alignment)
- Impact of Op-Amp: Provides Butterworth response, prevents loading by LP stage. If removed, 
  circuit becomes a passive RC filter with severe attenuation and soft roll-off (Q <= 0.5).


5. LOW-PASS (LP) SECTION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Component Breakdown:
- VR1A (10 kΩ Dual Pot, A): First variable series resistance for LP stage.
- R3 (1 kΩ): Minimum series resistance for first LP branch.
- VR1B (10 kΩ Dual Pot, B): Second variable series resistance for LP stage.
- R4 (1 kΩ): Minimum series resistance for second LP branch.
- R5 (220 Ω): Series stopper/limiting resistor placed before non-inverting input.
- C1 (10 nF) & C2 (10 nF): Connected in parallel from Node LP1 to Output Pin 7 (Total C_top = 20 nF).
- C3 (10 nF): Shunt capacitor connected from Node LP2 to Ground (C_gnd = 10 nF).
- U1B / NE5532: Unity-gain buffer (Pin 6 tied directly to Pin 7).

LP Signal Path:
HP Pin 7 -> [VR1A + R3] -> Node LP1 -> [VR1B + R4] -> Node LP2 -> [R5 (220Ω)] -> Pin 5 (+)
                            │                           │
                         [C1||C2]                      [C3]
                            │                           │
Audio_Output (Pin 7) <──────┴───────────── Pin 6 (-) <───┴─── Ground


6. LP FILTER OPERATION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Capacitive Reactance: Xc = 1 / (2 * pi * f * C)

Low Frequencies (f << fc):
- Capacitors act as open circuits (Xc -> ∞).
- Current flows directly through series resistors VR1A -> R3 -> VR1B -> R4 -> R5 into Pin 5.
- Output matches input voltage (Av = 1 V/V).

High Frequencies (f >> fc):
- Capacitors act as low-impedance shorts (Xc -> 0).
- C3 shunts high frequencies at Node LP2 to Ground.
- C1||C2 shunts high frequencies at Node LP1 back to op-amp output.
- Voltage reaching Pin 5 is attenuated at -12 dB/octave (-40 dB/decade).


7. LP CUTOFF FREQUENCY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Formula:
fc = 1 / (2 * pi * sqrt(R1 * R2 * C_top * C_gnd))

Parameters:
- R1 = VR1A + R3 = 1 kΩ to 11 kΩ
- R2 = VR1B + R4 + R5 = 1.22 kΩ to 11.22 kΩ
- C_top = C1 + C2 = 20 nF
- C_gnd = C3 = 10 nF

Calculations:
- Min LP Cutoff (VR1 = 10 kΩ, R1 = 11 kΩ, R2 = 11.22 kΩ):
  sqrt(R1 * R2) ≈ 11,109.5 Ω
  fc_min = 1 / (2 * pi * 11109.5 * sqrt(20nF * 10nF)) ≈ 1.01 kHz

- Max LP Cutoff (VR1 = 0 Ω, R1 = 1 kΩ, R2 = 1.22 kΩ):
  sqrt(R1 * R2) ≈ 1,104.5 Ω
  fc_max = 1 / (2 * pi * 1104.5 * sqrt(20nF * 10nF)) ≈ 10.18 kHz

Key Design Detail: C_top (20 nF) = 2 * C_gnd (10 nF) forces Q = 0.7071 (Butterworth response) 
in a unity-gain Sallen-Key LP topology.


8. POTENTIOMETERS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Potentiometer Structure: 3-terminal device (Track end 1, Wiper 2, Track end 3).
- Rheostat Wiring: Wiper is tied to one outer terminal. This converts it to a 2-terminal 
  variable resistor and prevents open-circuit faults if wiper contact flickers.
- Dual-Gang Control: Mechanical shaft links section A and section B so both filter poles 
  shift together, preserving response shape (Q).
- Schematic Flags:
  1. Duplicate labels: Both HP and LP dual pots are labeled "VR1A/VR1B". They are physically 
     two separate dual potentiometers (50 kΩ for HP, 10 kΩ for LP).
  2. Taper requirement: Must use Logarithmic (Audio) taper for linear frequency feel.


9. OP-AMP BUFFER / FOLLOWER (LP STAGE)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Configuration: Voltage follower / Unity-gain buffer (Pin 6 tied to Pin 7).
- Gain: Av = 1 V/V (0 dB).
- Input Impedance: Very high (~300 kΩ diff / >100 MΩ CM). Prevents loading on filter capacitors.
- Output Impedance: Very low (<0.1 Ω closed-loop).
- Purpose: Isolates LP filter network from external output cables, power amplifiers, or loads.

------|------------------------------|-------------------|-------------------|-------------------
N1    | Audio_Input, C1              | Full input        | Full input        | Full input
N2    | C1, C2, R3                   | Attenuated        | Unattenuated      | Unattenuated

11. FREQUENCY RESPONSE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Very Low Frequencies (< 10 Hz): Attenuated at -12 dB/octave by HP stage.
- HP Cutoff (19 Hz - 189 Hz): -3 dB relative to passband peak (+0.84 dB absolute), +90° phase shift.
- Passband Region (HP fc << f << LP fc): Constant gain Av = 1.556 (+3.84 dB), 0° phase shift.
- LP Cutoff (1.0 kHz - 10.2 kHz): -3 dB relative to passband peak (+0.84 dB absolute), -90° phase shift.
- High Frequencies (> 20 kHz): Attenuated at -12 dB/octave by LP stage. Phase lags toward -180°.
- Combined System: Variable Bandpass Filter (Variable subsonic rumble cut + Variable treble roll-off).


12. MATHEMATICAL VERIFICATION & SCHEMATIC FLAGS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Summary Values:
- HP Cutoff Range: 19.1 Hz to 189.4 Hz
- LP Cutoff Range: 1.01 kHz to 10.18 kHz
- HP Stage Gain: 1.556 V/V (+3.84 dB), Q ≈ 0.707
- LP Stage Gain: 1.000 V/V (0.00 dB), Q ≈ 0.707

Schematic Errors / Inconsistencies:
1. IC Identifier: Both op-amps are labeled `U1B NE5532`. Should be `U1A` (pins 1,2,3) and `U1B` (pins 5,6,7).
2. Designator Duplications: VR1A, VR1B, R3, R4, R5, C1, C2 are reused in both stages despite different values.
3. Parallel Capacitors: C1 (10 nF) and C2 (10 nF) in LP stage are parallel; can be a single 20 nF capacitor.
4. Supply Pins: Vcc/Vee supply decoupling pins (8 and 4) are omitted from the diagram.


13. PHYSICAL INTUITION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Capacitor Mechanics: High frequencies cause fast charge sloshing across plates, making 
  capacitors act as AC short circuits. Low frequencies cause full charge build-up, blocking current.
- Filtering Action: HP series caps block DC/lows while passing highs. LP shunt caps short 
  highs to ground/output while passing lows through series resistors.
- Active Feedback: Op-amp injects energy back into the RC node near cutoff to eliminate 
  droop and sharpen the filter transition knee.


14. DESIGN THINKING (STEP-BY-STEP)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Define Specs: Select cutoff ranges (e.g., HP 20-200 Hz, LP 1-10 kHz) and Butterworth alignment (Q=0.707).
2. Choose HP Caps: Set C = 150 nF.
3. Calculate HP Resistors:
   R_min = 1 / (2 * pi * 190Hz * 150nF) = 5.6 kΩ
   R_total = 1 / (2 * pi * 19Hz * 150nF) = 55.6 kΩ -> R_pot = 50 kΩ
4. Set HP Gain: Av = 3 - (1 / Q) = 1.586 V/V. Choose R5 = 1 kΩ, R6 = 1.8 kΩ (Av = 1.556 V/V).
5. Choose LP Caps: C_gnd = 10 nF, C_top = 2 * C_gnd = 20 nF (forces Q = 0.707 at unity gain).
6. Calculate LP Resistors: R_min = 1 kΩ, R_pot = 10 kΩ.
7. Buffer: Add unity-gain follower to eliminate downstream loading.


15. PRACTICAL ENGINEERING CONSIDERATIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Op-Amp Selection: NE5532 is ultra-low noise (5 nV/√Hz) and high GBW (10 MHz).
- Capacitors: Use 5% film capacitors (Polypropylene or C0G/NP0 ceramic). Avoid high-K ceramics.
- Power Supply: Requires dual supply (±12V to ±15V) with 100 nF decoupling caps at pins 8 and 4.
- Headphone Loading: Output current limit is ~38 mA. Connecting 32 Ω headphones directly 
  will cause severe current clipping. Requires a dedicated high-current output buffer (e.g. BUF634).
